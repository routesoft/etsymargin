Canada's fee structure is closer to the US model than the UK's — there's no regulatory operating
fee or VAT-on-fees charge — but the payment processing rate depends on whether the buyer is
paying from inside or outside Canada.

## Canadian fee schedule

| Fee | Rate | Applies to |
|---|---|---|
| Listing fee | $0.20 USD-equivalent | Per listing |
| Transaction fee | 6.5% | Item price + shipping |
| Payment processing (domestic) | 3% + C$0.25 | Full order total, Canadian buyer |
| Payment processing (international) | 4% + C$0.25 | Full order total, non-Canadian buyer |

## Worked example: a C$40 domestic sale

Item priced at C$35 with C$5 shipping, sold to a Canadian buyer.

| Line | Amount |
|---|---|
| Order total | C$40.00 |
| Listing fee | C$0.20 (≈USD) |
| Transaction fee (6.5%) | C$2.60 |
| Payment processing (3% + C$0.25) | C$1.45 |
| **Total Etsy fees** | **C$4.25** |

That's about **10.6%** of the order — close to the US rate, since Canada doesn't carry the extra
regulatory and VAT charges that push UK and EU fees higher.

## The domestic vs international difference matters more than it looks

Most Canadian Etsy shops sell to a mix of Canadian and US/international buyers. If a large share
of your sales are international, your *blended* effective fee rate creeps up from 3% to nearer 4%
on the processing side — worth tracking separately if you're deciding whether to run ads
targeted at Canadian buyers specifically.

## GST/HST is separate from Etsy's fees

Since Canada treats online marketplaces as facilitators, Etsy generally collects and remits
GST/HST on sales to Canadian buyers directly, rather than requiring the seller to track and remit
it themselves for those transactions. This doesn't change your Etsy *fees* — it's a tax
collected from the buyer and passed to the government, not a cost to you — but it does mean the
number showing as "order total" to the buyer may differ from the pre-tax price you set. Check
Etsy's current seller handbook for your specific province, since rules and thresholds can shift.

## Offsite Ads threshold in CAD

The $10,000 USD trailing-revenue threshold that drops the Offsite Ads fee from 15% to 12% is
denominated in USD, so the equivalent CAD figure moves with the exchange rate — budget with a
buffer rather than assuming a fixed CAD number.
