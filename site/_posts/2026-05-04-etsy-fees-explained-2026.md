Etsy doesn't charge one fee — it charges four or five, stacked on top of each other, and each
one behaves differently. That's the actual reason a shop can be busy and still not know if it's
profitable: nobody adds up all four in their head while listing a product.

Here's every fee, in the order they hit a typical US sale.

## 1. Listing fee — $0.20

Charged the moment you publish or renew a listing, regardless of whether it sells. A listing
stays live for four months or until it sells, then renews automatically for another $0.20. If
one listing sells ten times, you pay ten separate $0.20 renewal charges over its life — small
per sale, but it adds up for high-volume, low-price shops.

## 2. Transaction fee — 6.5%

Charged on your **order total**, not just the item price. That means the shipping and any gift
wrap you charge the buyer are included in the 6.5% calculation. Sell a $40 item with $8
shipping, and Etsy takes 6.5% of $48 ($3.12) — not 6.5% of $40.

## 3. Payment processing fee — 3% + $0.25 (US)

Charged on every transaction that runs through Etsy Payments, which is mandatory in most
countries. In the US it's 3% of the order total plus a flat $0.25. Outside the US, the flat and
percentage parts change — see our [UK](/blog/etsy-fees-for-uk-sellers/),
[Canada](/blog/etsy-fees-for-canadian-sellers/) and
[Australia](/blog/etsy-fees-for-australian-sellers/) breakdowns.

## 4. Offsite Ads fee — 12% or 15% (conditional)

Etsy sometimes advertises your listings on Google, Facebook, Instagram and Pinterest. If a
buyer clicks one of those ads and purchases within 30 days, you're charged 15% of the order
(shops earning under $10,000 in trailing 12 months can opt out) or 12% (mandatory once you
cross $10,000). It's capped at $100 per order. Full detail in our
[Offsite Ads guide](/blog/etsy-offsite-ads-fee-explained/).

## Everything else, briefly

- **Regulatory operating fee** — UK and EU sellers only, a small percentage on top of the order total.
- **VAT on fees** — UK and EU sellers without a VAT number pay VAT on Etsy's own fees, not just on sales.
- **Currency conversion** — 2.5% if your listing currency differs from your bank account's currency.
- **Etsy Plus** — an optional $10/month subscription with listing credits and ad credit, not a mandatory fee.
- **Shop setup fee** — a one-time $15–29 charge for new shops opened since September 2024.

## What it adds up to

On a typical US sale with no offsite ad involved, expect roughly **9–11% of your order total**
to go to Etsy before materials, labor or shipping cost. Add an offsite ad conversion and that
jumps to 21–26%.

## The one thing that trips up new sellers

The transaction and processing fees both apply to shipping you charge the buyer. Pricing
"free shipping" into the item price doesn't reduce the fee — it just moves which line item the
6.5% is calculated against. The fee is unavoidable either way; what matters is whether your
*total* price (item + shipping) covers materials, real shipping cost, and Etsy's cut with room
left for profit.
