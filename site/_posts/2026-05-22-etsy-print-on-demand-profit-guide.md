Print-on-demand looks simple on the surface — upload a design, set a price, the provider prints
and ships. The profit math has one extra layer than handmade selling, though: you're paying the
POD provider's base cost *and* Etsy's fees on top of it.

## The two-layer cost stack

1. **POD base cost** — what Printify, Printful or your provider charges to produce and ship
   the item. This varies by product: a t-shirt might be $8–13 base cost, a mug $6–9, a poster
   $10–18 depending on size.
2. **Etsy fees** — the same $0.20 listing fee, 6.5% transaction fee, and payment processing
   that every Etsy seller pays, calculated on your *selling* price, not the base cost.

## Worked example: a POD t-shirt

Say your POD base cost (production + provider's shipping) is **$11.50**, and you list the shirt
at **$24.99** with **$0** shipping charged (baked into price).

| Line | Amount |
|---|---|
| Selling price | $24.99 |
| Listing fee | $0.20 |
| Transaction fee (6.5%) | $1.62 |
| Payment processing (3% + $0.25) | $1.00 |
| Total Etsy fees | $2.82 |
| POD base cost | $11.50 |
| **Net profit** | **$10.67** |
| **Margin** | **42.7%** |

That's a healthy margin — but it depends entirely on pricing well above base cost, which is
where a lot of new POD sellers underprice, especially when checking competitor listings that
may already be running at thin or negative margins.

## Where POD sellers actually lose money

- **Copying a competitor's price** without knowing their base cost, which might be lower due to
  bulk provider discounts you don't have access to yet.
- **Ignoring provider shipping increases.** POD base costs change more often than most sellers
  check — a provider price bump silently erodes a margin that was fine three months ago.
  Recheck your numbers whenever the provider updates pricing.
- **Underestimating design/mockup time** as a sunk, one-time cost rather than something that
  needs to be recouped across enough sales of that design.
- **Offsite ads on thin-margin products.** A 12–15% ads fee on a product already sitting at
  20% margin can turn a "profitable" sale into a near-breakeven one.

## A pricing rule of thumb

A common starting point: price at 2–2.5× your POD base cost. It's not a guarantee of a healthy
margin — check the actual number — but it's a reasonable first pass before you validate with
real Etsy fee math for your specific numbers.
