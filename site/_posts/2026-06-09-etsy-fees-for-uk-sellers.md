UK sellers pay everything US sellers pay, plus two charges that don't exist in the US: a
regulatory operating fee and VAT on Etsy's fees. Both are easy to miss because Etsy doesn't
surface them as prominently as the headline transaction fee.

## UK fee schedule at a glance

| Fee | Rate | Applies to |
|---|---|---|
| Listing fee | $0.20 (charged in USD equivalent) | Per listing |
| Transaction fee | 6.5% | Item price + shipping + gift wrap |
| Payment processing | 4% + £0.20 | Full order total |
| Regulatory operating fee | ~0.4% | Item price + shipping + gift wrap |
| VAT on fees | 20% | The total fee amount, if not VAT-registered |

## Worked example: a £30 order

You sell an item for £25 with £5 shipping, not VAT-registered.

| Line | Amount |
|---|---|
| Order total | £30.00 |
| Listing fee | £0.16 (≈$0.20) |
| Transaction fee (6.5%) | £1.95 |
| Payment processing (4% + £0.20) | £1.40 |
| Regulatory operating fee (≈0.4%) | £0.12 |
| Subtotal fees | £3.63 |
| VAT on fees (20%) | £0.73 |
| **Total Etsy fees** | **£4.36** |

That's roughly **14.5%** of the order total — notably higher than the US's typical 9–11%,
almost entirely because of the processing rate difference, the regulatory fee, and VAT stacking
on top of everything else.

## If you're VAT-registered

VAT-registered sellers generally don't pay the 20% VAT-on-fees charge, or can reclaim it,
since it's treated as a business input cost. If you're near the UK VAT threshold, registering
earlier than required can sometimes make sense purely to recover this fee — though that's a
decision worth checking with an accountant, since VAT registration has other implications for
pricing and paperwork.

## Why the effective UK rate looks worse than the headline 6.5%

Etsy's marketing consistently cites the 6.5% transaction fee, which is accurate but incomplete.
UK sellers should budget for roughly **13–15% total fees** on a typical order once processing,
the regulatory fee and VAT are included — not 6.5%.

## A note on currency

Etsy bills the $0.20 listing fee in USD-equivalent regardless of your local currency, so the
exact GBP amount fluctuates slightly with exchange rates. It's a minor factor compared to the
percentage-based fees, but worth knowing if your own tracking doesn't match Etsy's statement to
the penny.
