## The short version

The calculator on this site runs entirely in your browser. The numbers you type in — price,
costs, shipping — are never sent to a server, stored, or seen by us. Closing the tab clears
them.

## What we do collect

Like most websites, our hosting and analytics setup may collect standard technical information
when you visit:

- Pages viewed and time spent on them
- Approximate location (country/region level), based on IP address
- Browser type and device type
- Referring site (e.g. if you arrived from a search engine)

This is aggregate, anonymized usage data used to understand which content is useful and to fix
broken pages. It is not used to identify you personally.

## Cookies

We use cookies only if an analytics tool is enabled on this site, purely to distinguish unique
visits. We don't use cookies for advertising retargeting or to track you across other websites.

## Third-party links

This site links to Etsy's fee schedule and other external resources for reference. We aren't
responsible for the privacy practices of sites we link to — check their own policies.

## Contact form / email

If you email us through the [contact page](/contact/), we keep that correspondence only as long
as needed to respond to you, and we don't add your email to any marketing list without asking
first.

## Children's privacy

This site is intended for business owners and isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't
knowingly collect data from them.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially, we'll update the date at the top of this page.

## Questions

Reach us any time via the [contact page](/contact/).
