Australia's Etsy fee structure sits between the US and UK models: no regulatory operating fee
like the UK, but GST does apply to Etsy's own fees, and payment processing splits by domestic vs
international buyer.

## Australian fee schedule

| Fee | Rate | Applies to |
|---|---|---|
| Listing fee | $0.20 USD-equivalent | Per listing |
| Transaction fee | 6.5% | Item price + shipping |
| Payment processing (domestic) | 3% + A$0.25 | Full order total, Australian buyer |
| Payment processing (international) | 4% + A$0.25 | Full order total, non-Australian buyer |
| GST on Etsy's fees | 10% | The fee amount, not the sale |

## Worked example: an A$45 domestic sale

Item priced at A$38 with A$7 shipping, sold to an Australian buyer.

| Line | Amount |
|---|---|
| Order total | A$45.00 |
| Listing fee | A$0.20 (≈USD) |
| Transaction fee (6.5%) | A$2.93 |
| Payment processing (3% + A$0.25) | A$1.60 |
| Subtotal fees | A$4.73 |
| GST on fees (10%) | A$0.47 |
| **Total Etsy fees** | **≈A$5.20** |

That's about **11.6%** of the order — a little higher than the US baseline mainly because of
GST on the fee amount, but well below the UK's typical 14–15% since Australia doesn't have an
equivalent to the UK's regulatory operating fee.

## GST registration and your own sales

If you're registered for GST (mandatory in Australia once you cross the AU$75,000 annual
turnover threshold), you handle GST on your own sales separately from what Etsy charges on its
fees — the two aren't the same GST calculation, and getting them confused can throw off your
BAS reporting. If you're below the threshold and not registered, this generally doesn't affect
your Etsy fee side of things.

## International sales are common — and slightly costlier

Australia's Etsy seller base sells heavily to US and UK buyers, where the international payment
processing rate (4% instead of 3%) applies. If most of your revenue comes from overseas buyers,
your blended fee rate will run a bit higher than the domestic-only example above — worth
checking your actual sales mix rather than assuming the domestic rate applies to everything.

## Currency conversion on top

If your listings are priced in AUD but a portion of your payout gets converted for any reason,
the standard 2.5% currency conversion fee can apply on top of everything above — check your
payment account settings if this is relevant to your setup.
