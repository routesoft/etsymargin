How the math works

Two of Etsy's charges are fixed per order — the listing fee and the flat part of payment processing — while the transaction fee and the percentage part of processing scale with your order total, including whatever shipping you charge. That mix means your effective fee rate falls as order value rises: the fixed costs matter a lot on a $6 sale and barely register on a $60 one.

Shipping is the other place profit quietly leaks. If you charge less for shipping than it actually costs you, the shortfall comes straight out of margin — and Etsy still charges its transaction fee on the amount you charged the buyer, not what you paid the carrier.

The formula

Order total = Price + Shipping charged Etsy fees = Listing fee + (Transaction rate × Order total) + (Processing rate × Order total + Processing flat fee) + Regulatory fee (UK/EU) + VAT on fees (UK/EU, if not VAT-registered) + Offsite ads fee (if applicable) Net profit = Order total − Etsy fees − Materials − Shipping cost Margin % = (Net profit ÷ Order total) × 100
