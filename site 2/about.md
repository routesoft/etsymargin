<section class="wrap page-body prose">

Most Etsy sellers find out how much they actually made the hard way — after the sale, when the
payout lands and it's smaller than expected. Etsy's own Profit Calculator is useful but tucked
away inside listing setup, and most third-party versions are attached to a print-on-demand
company or an SEO subscription with its own agenda.

EtsyMargin does one thing: it shows you what Etsy keeps and what you keep, before you set a
price, for shops in the US, UK, Canada, Australia and the Eurozone.

## Why the numbers are accurate

Every fee rate in the calculator is checked directly against Etsy's published
[fee and payments policy](https://www.etsy.com/legal/fees). US and UK rates are the most
precise, since Etsy documents them in full. Rates for other regions use representative figures
for the regulatory operating fee and VAT, since these can vary slightly by exact country — the
[country guides on the blog](/blog/) go into more detail where it matters.

We update the calculator when Etsy changes its fee structure. If you spot a rate that looks out
of date, [tell us](/contact/) and we'll check it.

## What EtsyMargin isn't

It isn't affiliated with Etsy, Inc. It doesn't sell your data, require an account, or connect to
your shop. Nothing you type into the calculator leaves your browser — the math runs entirely on
your device.
